# Channels

LinkedIn messages, emails, WhatsApp and Instagram for the same people belong in one sequence, one view and one launch. All channels share one contact history, so a reply, bounce or unsubscribe on one channel stops the others.

WhatsApp and Instagram use connected own Unipile accounts. Ramp sends and honor account caps. Ban risk exists. WhatsApp needs a phone column in E.164 format. Instagram needs an Instagram handle. Use WhatsApp only for warm or opted-in contacts. Use Instagram for creators or contacts with handles. A reply on either channel stops enrollment.

Never use one channel to route around a stop, a suppression, a blocked sender or a decline on another. An existing conversation is answered in that conversation, not by adding a sequence step.
