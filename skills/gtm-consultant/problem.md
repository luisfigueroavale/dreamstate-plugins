# Problem statement

Frame the diagnosis as situation, complication, and question. Situation is the snapshot. Complication is the gap between the goal and the trajectory, with both numbers. The question is the one this engagement answers.

Hold two or three hypotheses. For "replies but no meetings" the split is: replies are not positive (offer or message), positive replies sit unanswered (conversion), or the repliers fail Required ICP criteria (list). `positive_replies` against `replied`, unanswered interested threads through [`inbox-replies`], and a row sample separate them.

Rates say where. A short sample says why: recent replies, the last stalled or lost deals, and a few best customers. A lost-deal label is not a targeting rule until the conversations agree.

Ask why until someone in this workspace can act. "Reply rate is low" is not a cause. "The Required title filter keeps every title at the account" is a cause. Counterfactual: if this were fixed, would the goal metric move enough? If not, it contributes and it is not the constraint. Bounces are proximate. Buying unverified addresses or skipping warm-up is the root. Fix the root, and say what a pause buys meanwhile.

## Paragraph

```text
<Who> cannot reach <goal metric and date> because <root cause>,
shown by <two or three reads>.
If we fix it, <leading metric> moves from <now> to <target> inside <read window>.
We are not solving <out of scope> now.
```

Ask whether this matches what the user sees. Use the correction at once. Show the provisional recommendation in the same answer so the user is not waiting on a second round. Record the agreed paragraph, with the kill line as its reversal condition, through [`knowledge`].

One question per turn, and only when the answer changes the plan. Offer your recommended answer beside it.
