# Motion design

One canvas for this company. Each stage names the workspace object, not a slogan.

| Stage | Fill |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | Required criteria, and which Nice to have criteria are optional |
| Offer | The sentence a buyer could repeat, plus proof |
| Channel | One primary channel for the pilot |
| List | Worksheet source, or records already in [`crm`] |
| Message | Fixed scaffold, small personalized slots. Copy rules live in the channel skill. |
| Sender | What [`deliverability`] must show before the first send |
| Replies | Who answers, and how fast |
| Building block | Column, sequence, post, or record update. Smallest one that does the job. |

Search [`plays`] by the constraint, not by the channel. Open at most two. Write three lines: what you borrowed, what you changed, and why this company is different.

## Options

Two to four options that differ in kind (work the inbox, fix the list, change the angle, add a channel). Do not offer four subject lines. Compare impact, confidence, weekly hours, time until the metric can be read, whether the executing skill can preview cost, and reversibility. Recommend the shortest time to a signal among options rated medium or better on impact and confidence. State the strongest case against that pick and the free read that would change it.

Back-solve the goal through the funnel with the rates you actually read. If the math cannot hit the goal inside the team's hours, say that the goal, the rate, or the capacity has to move. Do not promise the gap away.
